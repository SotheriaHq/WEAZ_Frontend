### 3B. Production Timeline Extensions on Custom Bespoke Orders

A bespoke commission carries a production deadline and a delivery deadline, recorded on your order. For a new subscription-queue purchase, the production terms are captured at checkout and the deadlines run from activation; historical dates are preserved. Where a Brand cannot meet the production deadline, it may ask you — and only you — to grant additional time. The following limits are enforced by the platform and are not subject to negotiation between you and the Brand:

* **Your consent is required.** No extension takes effect unless you accept it. A Brand cannot extend its own deadline, and WIEZ does not extend it on a Brand's behalf.
* **Maximum per request**: three (3) calendar days.
* **Maximum number of requests**: two (2) approved extensions per order.
* **Maximum total extension**: six (6) calendar days per order. Requests you decline, or that expire unanswered, do not count against these limits.
* **Rush orders are never extended.** Where you have paid a rush production fee, the Brand is contractually barred from requesting additional time on that order. The rush fee purchases a date, not a priority.
* **Your response window**: twenty-four (24) hours from the request, or the original deadline, whichever falls first.
* **Silence is not consent.** If you do not respond before the window closes, the request expires, **no additional time is granted**, and the order is escalated to WIEZ for administrative review.
* **What acceptance does**: your production, dispatch and delivery dates each move by the number of days you granted, and your 72-hour inspection window (Section 5) and escrow release schedule move with them. The dates originally promised to you are retained on the order record and remain available to you, to the Brand, and to WIEZ.
* **What declining does**: nothing changes about your order, your payment, or your delivery date. Declining is **not** a cancellation and is **not** a refund request. It opens a dispute under Section 7 and places the order under WIEZ administrative intervention, in which WIEZ may contact you and the Brand separately to resolve the delay.
* **Record keeping**: every request, your response, any comment either party attached, the days granted and the resulting dates are recorded on the order and retained in accordance with Section 9 of the Privacy Policy.

Nothing in this Section limits your rights under Section 7 (Dispute Resolution) where a Brand fails to deliver within the extended timeline.

### 3C. Disputing a Late Custom Order

A dispute is not only for something that arrived and is wrong. Where a bespoke commission misses the dates recorded on it, you may escalate without waiting for delivery:

* **Production overdue**: available **24 hours** after the production date passes without the order advancing. Within that grace period the right action is to message your maker, and the app will offer that instead — makers run an afternoon late and usually say so.
* **Delivery overdue**: available **immediately** once the promised delivery date passes without delivery.
* **No photograph is required.** A complaint about an item that has not arrived cannot have one, and we do not ask for it.
* **Your order continues.** Raising a delay dispute does **not** cancel your order, does not stop the maker working, and is not itself a refund request. The remedy most shoppers want is the garment, and the maker is told to keep going unless WIEZ says otherwise.
* **Escrow freezes.** No funds are released to the Brand, and the automatic release timers are suspended, while the dispute is open.
* **WIEZ steps in.** The order is placed under administrative review. WIEZ may contact you and the Brand separately and privately; those messages are informational and are not a reply channel.
* **If the garment arrives while the dispute is open**, the dispute stays open and the question becomes whether you accept it late or seek a remedy. **You may close the dispute yourself at any time** — "accept it late and move on" — which ends the review and releases the order to settle normally.
* **One open dispute per order**, and a **48-hour** cooling-off period before the same order can be disputed again after a resolution.

## 5. Mandatory 72-Hour Inspection Window & 7-Day Administrative Auto-Release

Upon verified carrier delivery to your designated shipping address, our dual-tier inspection and auto-release mechanics protect both buyer dispute rights and merchant settlement certainty:

1. **72-Hour Buyer Inspection Window**: You have seventy-two (72) hours from the verified carrier drop-off timestamp to inspect your garment, try on bespoke fits, and verify fabric/craftsmanship.
2. **Filing a Dispute**: If an issue exists, you must tap **"Report an Issue / Dispute"** in the order screen or email `disputes@wiez.com` before the 72-hour window expires, attaching clear photographic evidence. This immediately freezes escrow disbursement.
3. **Buyer Early Confirmation**: If you are delighted with the delivered item, you may tap **"Confirm Delivery & Accept Fit"** at any time to release escrow funds immediately to the designer.
4. **Administrative 7-Day Maximum Auto-Release (`DEFAULT_SEED_AUTO_RELEASE_DAYS = 7`)**: To ensure merchant cashflow certainty when buyers fail to open the app or confirm delivery, platform settlement engines automatically finalize and release funds to the seller seven (7) calendar days following verified carrier delivery, provided no dispute has been lodged during the initial inspection period. Once released, claims must be directed to merchant warranty support.

## 6. Sizing, Fit Profiles, and Bespoke Fit Guarantees

### 6.1. Ready-to-Wear Sizing
Ready-to-wear listings include brand-specific sizing charts adhering to ISO 8559 standards. Buyers are responsible for selecting the appropriate size variant based on their measurements.

### 6.2. Bespoke Tailoring Fit Tolerance Guarantee (±0.75 Inch Rule)
For custom bespoke apparel, WIEZ guarantees precision tailoring:
* **The ±0.75 Inch Standard**: Finished custom garments must match the measurements recorded in your approved Custom Order Specification to within **±0.75 inches (1.9 cm)** across critical dimensions (bust/chest, waist, hips, sleeve length, inseam);
* **Fit Discrepancy Remedy**: If a custom garment deviates beyond ±0.75 inches due to tailor error, the Brand is legally obligated to perform **free alterations** (including covered shipping) or accept a full return and escrow refund;
* **Buyer Measurement Errors**: If a fit discrepancy results from inaccurate measurements submitted by the buyer, the Brand will perform alterations at a subsidized rate, with return shipping borne by the buyer.

## 7. Return, Replacement, and Refund Procedures

### 7.1. Grounds for Returns and Full Refunds
Buyers are entitled to return items and receive a 100% refund (including original shipping) under the following circumstances:
1. **Material Defect**: The garment arrives with torn seams, broken hardware, fabric tears, or chemical stains;
2. **Incorrect Item / Misrepresentation**: The item received differs significantly in style, cut, or fabric from the listing photography and description;
3. **Severe Fit Failure**: A custom bespoke garment exceeds the ±0.75 inch fit tolerance and the seller refuses or is unable to alter it;
4. **Non-Delivery**: The carrier fails to deliver the package within the guaranteed shipping SLA and tracking is lost.

### 7.2. Step-by-Step Return Process
1. **Tap "Request return"** in Order History, within 72 hours of delivery.
2. **Upload three clear photos** showing the defect or the size discrepancy.
3. **WIEZ issues a prepaid return shipping label** or arranges a courier pickup.
4. **The seller receives the item back** in its original, unworn condition.
5. **Your full refund is credited** to the original card or bank account within 5–10 business days.

### 7.3. Non-Returnable Items
For health, hygiene, and customized production reasons, the following items are non-returnable unless defective:
* Intimate apparel, swimwear, and bodysuits where the hygiene seal has been broken;
* Perfumes, cosmetic products, and customized fragrances;
* Worn, washed, soiled, or perfume-altered garments.

## Custom-order subscription queue terms

### Subscription custom-order policy scope and consumer rights

The subscription queue rules in this document apply to new custom-order queue purchases and subscription agreements shown and accepted under version `2026.10.19-v1.4`. Existing orders retain their recorded policy and payment snapshots; ready-to-wear rules are unchanged. Required notice of material changes remains applicable. Nothing here waives mandatory consumer rights, statutory refunds or access to the competent regulator or courts. Where mandatory law requires a different refund, that right prevails. [FCCPC consumer rights](https://fccpc.gov.ng/consumers/consumer-rights-responsibilities/rights-responsibilities/) explains cancellation rights and reasonable charges.

### Paid waiting queue, timing and priority

Custom-order payment is captured once at placement. Pending and Up Next funds remain held and unavailable as brand earnings until Active; Up Next is a notice and position, never a second garment charge. There is one Up Next seat. Active production and rush times begin at activation, not during waiting; queue position is not a delivery guarantee. Standard garment earnings follow the existing 60% production / 40% completion calculation, with no production release before Active and with existing dispute, verification and payout controls preserved. An internal held balance is an accounting restriction, not a representation of a separately protected bank escrow or provider deposit guarantee.

Ordinary Pending orders are FIFO. Only an Aru or Igwe brand that opts in may offer a paid jump. The separately disclosed fee is 20% of garment subtotal. It buys priority behind the current Up Next and earlier paid jumps, without displacing Up Next. When ordinary eligible orders are waiting, at most two paid-priority orders activate consecutively before one ordinary order. There is no paid jump offer when it provides no priority benefit. Of an earned jump fee, WIEZ receives 10% and the brand 90%; no brand share is earned before Active. Existing duplicate-source restrictions remain; ordering different items from one brand is permitted subject to ordinary checks.

Both Pending and Up Next expire 42 x 24 hours after committed paid placement. Cards and details show the cancellation countdown and exact deadline. One retain is permitted from 48 hours through exactly 12 hours remaining, inclusive, adding 42 days to the existing deadline while preserving priority. It is unavailable outside that window or after use, activation or cancellation. Shopper and brand reminders are scheduled at 48, 24 and 5 hours. **No outage grace applies:** failed notifications, unavailable notices or a WIEZ outage do not extend the cancellation deadline. The 5-hour notice does not reopen retain.

An admin may demote Up Next to Pending with a visible promotion hold. The hold stays until an admin releases it; it does not pause expiry, cancellation rights or the eligible retain window. Ordinary intake closure stops new purchases while already-paid obligations continue. Safety suspension stops promotion and unreleased payouts pending admin review. Removing a listing does not remove paid order snapshots or obligations. A capture completed after eligibility changes remains recorded and is resolved through the applicable cancellation/refund process.

### Cancellation charges, refund status and resubmission

Before Active, the shopper, brand, authorized admin or expiry process may cancel Pending or Up Next. **The same disclosed deductions apply to every such cancellation, including cancellation by the brand, admin or system:** the VAT actually recorded on the original payment, 10% of garment subtotal excluding VAT/delivery/jump fee, and 10% of any jump fee. Delivery is refunded in full and 90% of the jump fee is refunded. A missing VAT component means zero VAT deduction; this policy creates no VAT rate or new tax. There is no duplicate platform-share deduction on the jump fee and the brand earns no pre-Active jump share.

The server presents an itemized quote for confirmation and checks eligibility again before cancellation. Example: garment NGN100,000 + recorded VAT NGN7,500 + delivery NGN5,000 + jump NGN20,000 totals NGN132,500. Deductions total NGN19,500 and the refund is NGN113,000. This is an illustration using an existing VAT line, not a tax-rate promise. Mandatory statutory remedies prevail where applicable.

Cancellation records a refund request; it does not mean the bank has credited the shopper. Orders show requested, processing, processed, failed or attention-required status as appropriate. Provider processing and bank credit are distinct; bank receipt may take additional business days. Cancelling one line in mixed checkout does not cancel or refund sibling orders. No repeated request creates a second refund. After Active, existing dispute/admin cancellation, defect and delivery remedies apply.

Cancelled history, receipt, deductions, refund progress and permitted order messaging remain accessible. A resubmission is a separately priced new order at the back of the current queue, with current source, measurements, intake and payment checks. It may be placed while the previous refund is processing, but requires **a separate payment**; the pending refund is not wallet credit and continues independently. A new order does not inherit the old position or a free jump.

Brand subscription capacity and current entitlement are shown before purchase; see the [Seller Terms](/seller-terms) and [Payment Policy](/payment-policy) for the approved tier catalog, renewal and campaign rules.

### Original-payment conversion for custom-order refunds

When the original custom-order payment was converted into a different provider currency, its refund uses the original payment's recorded exchange-rate snapshot. WIEZ records the refund conversion, currency and provider amount for reconciliation; later exchange-rate changes do not reprice the refund. Shared-payment refunds use cumulative minor-unit rounding so their total cannot exceed the captured amount. If the original conversion cannot be verified, cancellation remains recorded and the refund requires reconciliation before provider submission. The refund quote identifies any estimated converted amount; provider processing and bank credit remain separate.

An agreed post-Active partial dispute refund returns only the agreed amount. The order continues in its existing production/delivery lane. Each refund is recorded separately, repeat requests cannot duplicate it, and any later full remedy refunds only the remaining refundable balance. Future milestone earnings are reduced for the refunded share; no held milestone becomes payable because of a partial refund.
