### 7.3. Artisanal Fit Tolerance Margin
* Handcrafted and bespoke tailoring involves natural manual cutting and textile flex.
* A completed custom garment that deviates within an industry-standard tolerance of **±0.75 inches (2.0 centimeters)** from the Shopper's submitted measurement profile is legally compliant, non-defective, and not eligible for return or chargeback on grounds of sizing defect.

### 7.4. Inspection Window & Alteration Protocol
* Upon delivery of a bespoke garment, the Shopper has **72 hours (the "Bespoke Inspection Window")** to inspect the fit and report material defects or sizing deviations exceeding standard tolerances via the order resolution drawer.
* If an inspection report proves that the Brand failed to adhere to the submitted measurement specifications, the Brand must, at its own expense: (a) execute necessary alterations within seven (7) business days, (b) manufacture a replacement garment, or (c) agree to an order cancellation and settlement refund.

### 7.5. Fabric Sourcing & Sourcing Variations
Where a custom order includes brand-sourced textiles, minor artisanal dye-lot variations, weave slubs, and display monitor color differences do not constitute actionable defects.
